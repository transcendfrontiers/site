Halo · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Halo is a personal angel number companion app. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Halo stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your date of birth, used solely to calculate your life path number
  • Your selected intentions and angel numbers
  • Journal entries you create (number sightings and notes)
  • Alert time preferences and notification settings
  • Your subscription status

All of this data is saved to your device's local storage. None of it is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above. Halo has no user accounts and no backend database.

Third-Party Services

Halo uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • RevenueCat: Handles in-app subscription purchases. RevenueCat may collect your Apple App Store receipt and purchase identifiers to validate subscriptions. See revenuecat.com/privacy for details.
  • Apple App Store: Subscription billing is processed entirely by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to all payment transactions.
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNs): Used to deliver angel number alerts you configure. No message content is stored by us.

Notifications

If you enable alerts, Halo schedules local notifications on your device. These are processed entirely on-device and are not routed through our servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling Halo removes all locally stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy

Halo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and release an app update. Continued use of Halo after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach us at hello@transcendfrontiers.com.
