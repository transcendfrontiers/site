Example

What an automation project can look like.

Say a home cleaning company gets a quote request through its website at 9pm. Here’s what happens next, step by step, before and after automation.

  1. 01

    Request comes in

    By hand · Next morning

    The form email sits in a shared inbox until someone checks it.

    Automated · Instant

    It goes straight into the CRM, tagged by service and zip code, and the owner gets a text.

  2. 02

    Details get logged

    By hand · 10 min

    Someone retypes the name, address and home size into the CRM and a spreadsheet.

    Automated · Instant

    The spreadsheet fills itself in from the CRM.

  3. 03

    Customer gets a quote

    By hand · 15 min

    The owner works out a price and writes the reply from scratch.

    Automated · Under a minute

    A reply with an estimate based on your rates goes out right away.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    By hand · 5 min

    If the customer goes quiet, someone has to remember to email again.

    Automated · After 2 days

    A friendly follow-up goes out after two days if there’s no answer.

  5. 05

    Weekly report

    By hand · 1 hr

    Friday afternoon goes to tallying up requests and bookings.

    Automated · Every Monday

    A summary email with the week’s numbers arrives Monday morning.

Time spent

Before

About 30 minutes, plus an hour every Friday.

After

A reply in under a minute, with nothing retyped by hand.

Browser agents that turn an owner’s questionnaire into finished listings on Vrbo, Zillow Rental Manager and TurboTenant, which reach Trulia, HotPads, Apartments.com, Realtor.com and more. About 3 hours of work per property, down to about 30 minutes.

Process

How a project works, from first call to handoff.

  1. 01

    Intro call or email

    We spend about 30 minutes talking through what you need and the tools you use now. If a call doesn’t suit you, we can do the whole thing over email instead.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    You get a written scope with the price and timeline before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    We share progress as we go, so you can give feedback early instead of at the end.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    You get the logins, documentation and a walkthrough of everything we built. We can also stay on for ongoing support.
