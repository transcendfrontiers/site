Example

What an automation project can look like.

Say a small business gets customer inquiries through a form on its website, and everything after that happens by hand.

Before

  • Form submissions get forwarded to a shared inbox.
  • Someone copies each inquiry into a spreadsheet and the CRM.
  • Follow-up emails are written one at a time.
  • The weekly summary is put together by hand on Friday.

After

  • Form submissions go straight into the CRM, already tagged.
  • The spreadsheet updates itself from the CRM.
  • A first reply goes out right away, and the owner gets a Slack message.
  • A summary email arrives every Monday morning.

Not sure what could be automated? Send us a short description of how the work gets done today, and we’ll tell you what we would change.

Process

How a project works, from first call to handoff.

  1. 01

    Intro call or email

    We spend about 30 minutes talking through what you need and the tools you use now. If a call doesn’t suit you, we can do the whole thing over email instead.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    You get a written scope with the price and timeline before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    We share progress as we go, so you can give feedback early instead of at the end.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    You get the logins, documentation and a walkthrough of everything we built. We can also stay on for ongoing support.
