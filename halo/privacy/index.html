Privacy

Privacy Policy

Halo keeps everything on your device. There's no backend, no user accounts, and no server-side storage. Here's what the app holds and why.

What we store

Everything below lives on your device only and never leaves it:

  • Your date of birth, used to calculate your life path number. Never sent anywhere.
  • Your selected intentions and angel numbers
  • Journal entries you log (number sightings and notes)
  • App settings like alert times, notification preferences, and time format

Subscription status is fetched from Apple and RevenueCat each time you open the app.

Photo library

If you save a share card, Halo asks to write one image to your photo library. It never reads photos you already have.

What we don't collect

No name, email, location, or contacts. Halo has no user accounts and no backend.

Analytics

Halo uses PostHog to see how the app is used: screen views, app opens, and a few in-app actions like scheduling an alert or logging a journal entry. These events are tied to a random device ID, not your name or an account, since Halo doesn't have accounts. Your journal notes, locations, and birthday are never sent to PostHog.

Tracking

Halo doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Your data is never sold or used for ads. The analytics above are anonymous, in-app usage stats, not cross-app tracking.

Third-party services

A few services are used to keep the app running:

  • RevenueCat: Handles subscription purchases. May collect your App Store receipt and purchase identifiers to validate your subscription. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store: All billing goes through Apple. Apple's privacy policy covers payment transactions.
  • Apple Push Notification Service: Delivers the angel number alerts you set up. No message content passes through our servers.
  • Sentry: Crash reporting. If the app breaks, Sentry may collect device model, OS version, app version, and a stack trace. Used only to fix bugs. No personal info is intentionally sent. sentry.io/privacy
  • PostHog: Product analytics. Collects anonymous usage events like screen views and alert or journal actions, tied to a random device ID. posthog.com/privacy

Notifications

Alerts are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing goes through our servers.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and all your data goes with it. That's it.

Your rights

Your data is on your device and you control it. California residents have the right to know what's collected and to request deletion. Since we don't hold anything on our end, the only copy is on your device. Questions? hello@transcendfrontiers.com.

Kids

Halo isn't for anyone under 13. If you think a child under 13 is using the app, reach out at hello@transcendfrontiers.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date here and push an app update. Keep using Halo after that and you're good with the new version.
