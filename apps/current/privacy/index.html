Current · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Current keeps everything on your device. There's no backend, no user accounts, and no server-side storage. Here's what the app holds and why.

What We Store

Everything below lives on your device only and never leaves it:

  • Your check-in history: the state you logged, the timestamp, and whether it came from the app or widget
  • Your personalization selections from onboarding (e.g. "work stress", "poor sleep") used to surface contextual prompts
  • Your notification time window preferences
  • Your widget's most recent state and a short history of up to 5 check-ins, stored in a shared app group container so the widget can read them

Subscription status is fetched from Apple and RevenueCat each time you open the app.

What We Don't Collect

No name, email, location, or contacts. Current has no user accounts and no backend.

Tracking

Current doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Your data is never sold or used for ads.

Third-Party Services

A few services are used to keep the app running:

  • RevenueCat: Handles subscription purchases. May collect your App Store receipt and purchase identifiers to validate your subscription. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store: All billing goes through Apple. Apple's privacy policy covers payment transactions.
  • Apple Push Notification Service: Delivers the reminders you set up. No message content passes through our servers.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing goes through our servers.

Deleting Your Data

Delete the app and all your data goes with it. That's it.

Your Rights

Your data is on your device and you control it. California residents have the right to know what's collected and to request deletion. Since we don't hold anything on our end, the only copy is on your device. Questions? hello@transcendfrontiers.com.

Kids

Current isn't for anyone under 13. If you think a child under 13 is using the app, reach out at hello@transcendfrontiers.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date here and push an app update. Keep using Current after that and you're good with the new version.

Contact

Questions? hello@transcendfrontiers.com.
