Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Fabric Lens is a Chrome extension that detects fabric and material composition on clothing product pages and displays a visual breakdown. This privacy policy explains what data is and is not collected when you use the extension.

Data We Do Not Collect

Fabric Lens does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information of any kind
  • Browsing history
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Information about which websites you visit
  • Any data about your device or location

How the Extension Works

When you open the Fabric Lens popup on a product page, the extension reads the visible text content of that page locally within your browser to detect fabric and fiber information. This processing happens entirely on your device. No page content is ever sent to any server.

The "Report This Page" Feature

The only instance where any data leaves your device is when you voluntarily click the "Report this page" or "Report incorrect" button. When clicked, the URL of the current page is sent to a private Google Sheet owned by the developer. This is used solely to identify product pages where the extension did not work correctly, so that detection can be improved.

This action is:

  • Entirely optional and user-initiated
  • Limited to the page URL only — no personal information is included
  • Not linked to any user identity or account

Data Storage

Fabric Lens uses Chrome's built-in session storage to temporarily cache detection results for the active tab. This data exists only for the duration of your browser session and is never transmitted externally.

Third-Party Services

When a report is submitted voluntarily, the page URL is received by a Google Apps Script web app and stored in a Google Sheet. This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

No other third-party services, analytics tools, or tracking services are used.
